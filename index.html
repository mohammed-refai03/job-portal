Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions from our applicants and recruiting firms.

How do I apply for jobs on StackLy?

To apply, simply create a free account, complete your career profile details, upload your CV PDF, search for jobs, and click "Apply Now". Your application will be sent directly to the employer's dashboard.

How long does recruiter profile verification take?

To protect candidates from spam, recruiter profiles go through verification checks. This process usually takes between 12 to 24 hours. Verification requires corporate domain email addresses.

Is StackLy free for job seekers in India?

Yes, search and application features are 100% free for candidates. We also provide basic resume-building tools at no charge. Premium memberships are optional for candidates seeking spotlight visibility or direct coaching.

Can I upload multiple CV versions?

Yes, you can upload and save up to 3 versions of your resume (e.g. customized for Frontend, Backend, or Product Manager roles) in your dashboard settings, choosing which to send when applying.
